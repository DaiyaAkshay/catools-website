26AS / AIS / TIS Downloader

One-click bulk download of Form 26AS, AIS, TIS — plus 143(1) intimation — from the Indian Income Tax e-Filing portal. Logs in for every client, unlocks the password-protected PDFs, and ships you a partywise + sectionwise Excel summary including refund + interest u/s 244A.

What it does

During filing season a CA logs into the IT portal dozens — sometimes hundreds — of times. For each client you punch in PAN, password, the OTP-style "secure access" prompt, the dual-login confirmation, walk through TRACES, tick the "I agree" checkbox, then export 26AS. Repeat for AIS, repeat for TIS, repeat for the intimation. Multiply by 80 clients. That's the afternoon this tool gives back to you.

You add your assessees once (manually or by Excel bulk-import). Then click Download. The tool drives your own installed Google Chrome — quietly, in the background — fills in credentials, navigates the portal, downloads the documents, strips their PDF passwords, and drops everything into a per-client folder in your Downloads. Because it's real Chrome, the portal treats each login as genuine rather than blocking it as an automated bot.

Headline features

  • Bulk login + download. 26AS, AIS, TIS, and the latest 143(1) intimation — all four with one click per assessee.
  • Auto PDF unlock. The IT department locks every PDF with DDMMYYYY or pan+DDMMYYYY. We unlock them for you using bundled qpdf.
  • Excel report — partywise. 26AS Part A rows grouped by deductor (TAN), totals per party.
  • Excel report — sectionwise. Same data grouped by TDS section (192, 194A, 194C, 194J …).
  • Intimation refund + 244A interest. Parses the 143(1) intimation PDF and pulls the refund determined, demand raised, and interest u/s 244A into a column on the master sheet.
  • All-clients master sheet. One row per assessee with TDS totals + refund/interest — perfect for partner review.
  • Bulk import from Excel. Onboard 100 clients in a minute with a 4-column sheet.
  • Encrypted at rest. PAN passwords are AES-encrypted using Windows DPAPI. Steal the file off the machine and it's unusable.
  • Auto-updates. The IT portal changes its layout often; updates ship to your installed app automatically.

What it's not

It is not a cloud service. There is no account, no server we hold your data on. The tool runs on your Windows PC; logins happen from your IP address using your own Chrome browser. Nothing about your clients touches our server.

It does not file returns, compute tax, or replace your filing software. It moves data from the portal to your local Excel — that's the entire scope.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • Google Chrome installed (the tool drives your real Chrome for portal logins)
  • Internet connection to incometax.gov.in and tdscpc.gov.in
  • ~400 MB free disk space for the installer

Frequently asked

How is this different from a free 26AS downloader?

Free scripts stop at downloading 26AS / AIS / TIS. CAtool also pulls the latest 143(1) intimation with refund + 244A interest, parses TDS partywise and sectionwise into a partner-ready Excel, and lives in the same hub as GSTR-2B Recon and compliance checks.

Will the portal block it as a bot?

No. It drives your real installed Google Chrome with the automation flags stripped, so the e-Filing portal sees a normal Chrome login — not a headless bot. The window runs hidden so it doesn't interrupt you.

Where do my clients' passwords go?

Nowhere. They're encrypted with Windows DPAPI on your own machine and used only to log in to the portal from your PC. No password or PAN is sent to us or written to logs.

What if the IT portal changes its layout?

It changes often. The app auto-updates — when the portal shifts, a fix reaches your installed copy within days, no reinstall needed.

Do I have to pay anything?

No. CAtool is completely free during early access — no card, no subscription. Paid plans may come later once the tool has proven its worth, but there's nothing to pay today.
