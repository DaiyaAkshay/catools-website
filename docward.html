Why on-device matters

Most “free” PDF tools upload your file to their servers to process it. For a CA, a lawyer, or anyone handling PANs, financials, contracts or medical records, that's the whole problem. Docward does the work on your PC — the file is opened, edited and saved without ever leaving your machine. It's not a promise you have to trust; the app is built so there's simply nowhere for your documents to go.
