Quick answers

Is it really free? No catch?

Yes. CAtool is completely free during early access — no card, no auto-charge, no subscription. We may introduce fair paid plans later, once the tools have proven their worth, but there's nothing to pay today.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Client PANs, IT-portal passwords, and downloaded PDFs never leave your Windows PC. Passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI — the same key your Windows login uses. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Yes. Install it on as many office PCs as you like — there's no seat count or licence to manage during early access.

It happens often. CAtool auto-updates — when the portal shifts, a fix reaches your installed app within days. No reinstall.
