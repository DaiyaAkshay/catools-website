GSTR-2B vs Books Reconciliation

Match every entry on your GSTR-2B against your books of accounts. Handles the messy reality — different column names from Tally / Busy / SAP, 2-row headers, credit notes, and the timing differences between portal and books.

Platform: Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) Status: Coming soon — in development Pricing: Free during early access
Get notified when it lands See the 26AS Downloader

What it does

Reads GSTR-2B (JSON download or Excel) and your books of accounts (any column layout — Tally, SAP, Busy, Excel ledger). Matches invoices on GSTIN + Invoice No first, falls back to amount matching when the invoice number differs. Tolerance is ₹1 by default.

Headline features

  • Smart Excel reader. Auto-detects the header row even when there are metadata rows above it, merges 2-row headers, tries all sheets.
  • Fuzzy column mapping. Hard aliases for common GST / Tally / Busy / SAP column names, plus a similarity-scored fuzzy fallback. Save your firm's mappings as a profile.
  • Unified invoice + note tabs. Credit / debit notes live alongside invoices in the same tab with a Doc Type column. Reconciliation engine separates and matches them on the correct keys.
  • Monthly + annual modes. Monthly mode supports carry-forward between periods; annual mode handles year-end GSTR-2B / 2A diffs.
  • Folder scan. Point the tool at one client folder. It classifies each file (2B JSON, 2B Excel, books, ledger, CDN), detects the client GSTIN, loads everything in one shot.
  • Combined-file import. One workbook with 4 sheets (2B / 2B-notes / books / books-notes) — the tool collapses it into the two unified tabs.
  • Compliance built-in. Section 16(4) ITC time-limit, Rule 37 (180-day non-payment), RCM register — all auto-computed after each reconciliation.
  • Electronic Credit + Cash Ledger. Year-end ITC and cash ledger reconciliation with the March / April-return timing roll-forward already wired up.

Why CAs use it

Most "GST recon" tools cost ₹500-2,000/month and force you into a fixed column schema. Our books data is never in that schema. This tool learns your column names once, then reconciles 12 months in 30 seconds.
