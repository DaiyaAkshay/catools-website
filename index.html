How it works →

Control CAtool from your phone

One partner stays in the office. Everyone else monitors progress and triggers downloads from wherever they are — phone, tablet, another laptop.

1

Install CAtool on any office Windows PC

That PC becomes the coordinator — it holds the encrypted client list and runs Chrome for the IT portal.

2

Open Settings → enable Office Sharing

CAtool starts a local hub server and shows a QR code. One scan pairs your phone instantly — no app store, no account.

3

Pick clients on your phone, tap Dispatch

Select one or fifty clients, choose 26AS / AIS / TIS / Intimation, and watch the progress update live. Jobs queue automatically — one client at a time, no portal conflicts.

4

Files land on the office PC as usual

PDFs and Excel reports save to your normal downloads folder. Everything stays on your machine — the phone only sends instructions, not data.

📱

No app store needed

CAtool Remote is a Progressive Web App — open the link on your phone's browser and tap "Add to Home Screen". Works like a native app, installs in 10 seconds.

Questions CAs ask

Is my clients' data safe?

Yes. CAtool is a desktop app — there is no cloud. Passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI on your own machine, documents download directly from the IT portal to your disk, and nothing is uploaded to us or any third party.

Do I need Google Chrome installed?

Only for the 26AS / AIS / TIS Downloader (it drives the IT portal). The GSTR-2B Recon and Tally Audit Dashboard work entirely offline against your own files and Tally data — no browser needed for those.

Does the GSTR-2B tool connect to Tally directly?

Yes — if TallyPrime is running on the same machine (or LAN), CAtool can pull your purchase register straight from Tally with one click. You can also load data from Excel if you prefer.

What happens when the IT portal changes its layout?

It happens often. Each tool updates independently in the background — when the portal shifts, a fix ships within days. No reinstall, no manual download.

What does it cost?

Nothing right now. CAtool is completely free during early access — no card, no subscription. We may introduce paid plans later once the tools have earned a place in your practice, but everything you download today stays usable.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. There is no Mac or Linux build today.
