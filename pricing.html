Frequently asked

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. CAtool is completely free during early access — no card at install, no auto-debit, no subscription. There's simply nothing to pay today.

Will it always be free?

We may introduce fair paid plans later, once the tools have matured and proven their value. We'll announce it well in advance — and whatever you've installed will keep working.

Can my whole office use it?
