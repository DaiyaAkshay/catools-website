Frequently asked

How does the trial work?

Install the app — it gives you 14 days of full functionality, no card details needed. After 14 days you can subscribe from inside the app.

What is UPI AutoPay?

A standing instruction on your UPI app to debit a small amount on a recurring date. ₹149 will be debited every month until you cancel. You can cancel the mandate from your UPI app (PhonePe / GPay / Paytm / BHIM) any time — no need to contact us.

Can I use it on more than one PC?

The activation key is bound to one device. If you change machines, email us — we'll re-issue the key to the new device. No extra charge.

What if the IT portal changes and the tool breaks?

It will happen. When it does, we ship an update within a few days and your installed app auto-updates. If a fix takes longer than a week, that month is on us — email us and we'll waive the next billing cycle.

Refunds?

See the refund and cancellation policy.

Do you store our clients' PAN passwords on your server?

No. Passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI and never leave your PC. The only thing our server sees is your subscription record (email + device ID).
