Frequently asked

How does the trial work?

Install the app — it gives you full functionality with no credit card required. If you install on or before 31 December 2026, you get a 3-month free trial as part of our launch offer. Installs after that date get the standard 14-day trial. In both cases, the timer starts the day you first open the app, and you can subscribe from inside the app any time before it expires.

What happens when my 3 months end?

The tools soft-block until you subscribe — no surprise charges, no auto-debit from a card we don't have. Your client list, settings, downloaded 26AS PDFs and any exported Excels stay on your machine; nothing is deleted. You can subscribe at ₹149/mo (single PC) or ₹499/mo (firm) any time and pick up where you left off.

What's the difference between Single and Firm tier?

Single (₹149) activates on one PC, identified by a hardware fingerprint. If a different PC tries the same key, it won't work.

Firm (₹499) activates against your office network — specifically your router's MAC address and your office public IP. Any PC connected to your office LAN can use the tool. A staff member taking the key home and using it on their personal Wi-Fi will be blocked. Office VPN users are treated as on-LAN, so working from home via the office VPN still works.

What is UPI AutoPay?

A standing instruction on your UPI app to debit a small amount on a recurring date. ₹149 (or ₹499) will be debited every month until you cancel. You can cancel the mandate from your UPI app (PhonePe / GPay / Paytm / BHIM) any time — no need to contact us.

Can I upgrade from Single to Firm later?

Yes. Cancel the ₹149 mandate from your UPI app, then subscribe to the Firm tier from the app or this page. We'll honor any remaining days from your old subscription.

My office's public IP changes — will the Firm tier break?

If your ISP changes your public IP (rare but possible), the desktop app shows a one-click "Re-link to this network" button. We verify via a magic link emailed to your subscription address, then reissue the key with the new IP. Takes ~30 seconds.

What if the IT portal changes and the tool breaks?

It will happen. When it does, we ship an update within a few days and your installed app auto-updates. If a fix takes longer than a week, that month is on us — email us and we'll waive the next billing cycle.

Do you store our clients' PAN passwords on your server?

No. Passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI and never leave your PC. The only thing our server sees is your subscription record (email + device ID for Single, or email + office network ID for Firm).
