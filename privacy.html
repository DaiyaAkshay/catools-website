Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how CA Akshay Daiya (sole proprietor, operating under the brand catool.co.in) handles your data. We treat this seriously because our customers are practising Chartered Accountants who themselves owe confidentiality duties to their clients.

1. Who we are

CA Akshay Daiya, partner at M/s Daiya Tiwari & Soni, Chartered Accountants, is the data controller for this site and the catool.co.in product line. Email: akshaybkn@gmail.com. Phone: +91 99290 89598.

2. What we collect

From this website and the subscription flow:

  • Your email address — to deliver the activation key and billing receipts.
  • Your PAN (optional) — for invoice records.
  • A device fingerprint (SHA-256 hash of hostname + MAC + platform) — to bind the licence key to one PC and prevent uncontrolled key sharing.
  • Your Razorpay subscription metadata (subscription ID, payment status, billing dates) — to know whether your key should remain active.
  • Standard web server logs (IP, user-agent, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention.

From the desktop applications (26AS Downloader, GSTR-2B Recon, Tally Audit):

  • Nothing. The desktop apps do not transmit assessee data, PAN passwords, books data, or any client information to us or any third party. Everything stays on your Windows PC.
  • The 26AS Downloader's automated login uses your client's PAN + password against the IT e-Filing portal directly from your machine — those credentials never traverse our servers.
  • When a new version is released, the app contacts GitHub Releases to download the installer. No personal data is transmitted with this check.

3. Why we collect it

  • Email + PAN: billing, support, and statutory invoice generation.
  • Device hash: licence enforcement (one paid licence = one PC).
  • Subscription metadata: to issue and revoke activation keys when your UPI mandate succeeds or is cancelled.

4. Who we share it with

  • Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. — to process your UPI AutoPay subscription. They receive your email, PAN, and payment instrument data. Refer to razorpay.com/privacy.
  • Our cloud host (Cloudflare Pages, Render) — they receive standard server logs.
  • Indian government authorities — if compelled by valid legal process under Indian law.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not run ad networks, analytics that profile users across sites, or marketing trackers beyond a single Google Analytics counter on this site.

5. How long we keep it

  • Active subscription records: for as long as the subscription is active, plus 8 financial years (Section 36 of the CGST Act & Section 44AA of the Income Tax Act book-keeping requirements).
  • Cancelled subscriptions: we retain the email + payment record for 8 financial years, then permanently delete.
  • Server logs: 30 days.

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act 2023

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Erase your personal data, subject to statutory retention requirements above.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by cancelling your subscription and emailing us to delete your account.
  • Lodge a grievance with the Data Protection Board of India if we don't respond satisfactorily.

Email all such requests to akshaybkn@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 7 working days.

7. Security

  • The licence server uses HTTPS only.
  • Stored payment data lives with Razorpay (a PCI-DSS compliant processor) — we do not store card or UPI VPA details on our infrastructure.
  • Desktop apps encrypt PAN passwords with Windows DPAPI (only decryptable on the same Windows user account on the same machine).

8. Children

Our products are professional CA tools and are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
