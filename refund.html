Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

Free trial first

Every paid product offers a free trial before any payment is taken. Installs on or before 31 December 2026 qualify for the launch-offer 3-month free trial; installs after that date get the standard 14-day trial. We strongly recommend exhausting the trial — and ideally running it against your real client list during a real filing cycle — before subscribing. The trial period gives you the full feature set with no functional limit.

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your UPI app by revoking the Razorpay AutoPay mandate. We cannot debit your bank account once you cancel.

  • Cancelling before the next billing date prevents the next ₹149 charge.
  • Your activation key remains valid until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.
  • You may also email us at akshaybkn@gmail.com from your registered email; we'll mark the subscription as cancelled and confirm in writing.

Refund eligibility

Because we offer a generous free trial, refunds on completed billing cycles are limited. We will issue a refund in these cases:

  • Service outage of more than 7 consecutive days caused by us — including a portal-compatibility break we fail to fix within that window. The full month's ₹149 will be refunded to your UPI VPA.
  • Accidental double-charge for the same billing cycle (Razorpay duplicates). Refunded within 5 working days.
  • Charge after a documented cancellation — i.e. you cancelled, Razorpay still charged. Refunded within 5 working days.

We will not issue refunds for:

  • "I didn't use it this month." The trial existed to test fit; we cannot rescind charges retroactively for non-use.
  • Issues caused by changes to the Income Tax portal, GST portal, or TRACES that we fix within 7 days.
  • Issues caused by your own incorrect credentials, expired passwords, or network problems.
  • Subscription periods that have already lapsed more than 30 days ago.

How to request a refund

Email akshaybkn@gmail.com from the address on your subscription. Include:

  • The Razorpay Subscription ID (looks like sub_XXXXXXX).
  • The transaction reference of the charge in question.
  • A brief description of why you believe a refund is due.

We respond to all refund requests within 3 working days. Approved refunds appear back in your UPI account within 5 working days of approval (subject to Razorpay's processing times).

Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback through your bank. We resolve 99% of issues quickly by email. Chargebacks initiated without a prior email cost us significant time and may delay a resolution you would otherwise get within days.
