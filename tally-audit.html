Tally Audit Dashboard Coming soon

An end-to-end audit platform for statutory + tax auditors. Reads your Tally backup, cross-references it against 26AS, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and shows you the exceptions. Currently in private beta with our own audit team.

What's planned

  • TDS / TCS section-wise recon. Tally ledgers grouped by section (192, 194A, 194C, 194J, 194Q …) and compared against 26AS at the section + PAN level.
  • PAN clubbing. Multiple Tally party ledgers that share a PAN get grouped automatically — catches the "Same party, three ledgers" mess.
  • GSTIN-vs-PAN consistency. Flags parties where the GSTIN's embedded PAN doesn't match the PAN on the ledger.
  • Sales register vs GSTR-1. B2B / B2C split, rate-wise summary, mismatches.
  • Exception reports. Round-trip transactions, ledger duplications, suspicious round-figure entries — the things partners want to see before signing.
